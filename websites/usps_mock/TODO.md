## P1 — Service workflows

- [x] **Account overview and service histories.** Home shows four service shortcuts and recent requests sorted by timestamp with stable ID tie-breaking. Each service page shows a labeled search, status filter, record count, newest-first history, accessible empty state, and primary create action. Search by confirmation or record ID and recognizable fields (mover/address, held address, claim tracking/item, or PO Box number/location). Distinguish no records from no matches. Search and filtering are view state.
- [x] **Change of address — submit.** Choose Individual, Family, or Business and Permanent or Temporary. Collect mover name (or business name), contact email, old/new U.S. mailing addresses, start date, and end date for temporary forwarding. Require different normalized addresses and a temporary term of 15–185 days. Preview addresses and forwarding dates and return a confirmation code linked to the detail. Append one `changeOfAddressRequests` record with `status: scheduled`, `moveType`, `forwardingType`, `moverName`, `contactEmail`, `oldAddress`, `newAddress`, `startDate`, `endDate`, and creation metadata.
- [x] **Change of address — edit and cancel.** For a scheduled record, offer date and destination corrections with the same validations, plus a separate cancellation confirmation. Keep the request ID and confirmation code. Edit only `newAddress`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy`. Confirmed cancellation sets `status: cancelled` and writes cancellation and update metadata on that record. The cancelled detail remains readable.
- [x] **Hold Mail — schedule.** Prefill the account mailing address when available. Confirm the complete address, choose an inclusive 3–30-day date range beginning from tomorrow through 30 days ahead, and select carrier delivery or Post Office pickup. Require at least three full intervening days between scheduled holds at the same normalized address. Display the chosen return method at review and confirmation. Append one `holdMailRequests` record with `status: scheduled`, `address`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `deliveryMethod`, computed `durationDays`, and creation metadata.
- [x] **Hold Mail — edit and cancel.** Open a scheduled hold from history and edit the date range or return method. Apply scheduling rules excluding the current record, recompute `durationDays`, and stamp update metadata. Confirm cancellation separately and set `status: cancelled` with cancellation and update metadata. Both actions update the original `holdMailRequests` record in place.
- [x] **Package claims — file a domestic claim.** Capture tracking number, mailing date, service, sender/recipient role and addresses, reason (lost, damaged, missing contents), claimed items, and evidence. Normalize tracking input to 13–34 alphanumeric characters. Allow lost claims at 7–60 days after mailing for Priority Mail Express or 15–60 for the other supported services. Allow damage and missing-contents claims through 60 days from mailing. Allow adding/removing item rows with description, positive integer quantity, and positive unit value. Calculate totals in cents. Collect proof of value and insurance, plus proof of damage for damage claims. Accept local PDF/JPEG/PNG selection and retain filename, type and byte size as metadata. Review the tracking, items, evidence and total before filing. Append one `packageClaims` record with `status: submitted`, shipment and claimant fields, `items[]`, `evidence[]`, `claimAmount`, `currency`, `submittedAt`, and creation metadata. Show confirmation details and the new history row.
- [x] **Package claims — save and resume a draft.** Add an explicit Save draft action after valid tracking input. Create a `packageClaims` record with `status: draft` and the fields entered so far. Drafts appear in history and open in a prefilled editable form. Later saves update the same record with update metadata. Filing a resumed draft validates every required field, keeps the original ID and `createdAt`, sets `status: submitted`, assigns a confirmation code, and writes `submittedAt` plus update metadata. Filed claims surface their status and details in a read-only view.
- [x] **PO Boxes — search and reserve.** Search the `postOffices` catalog by ZIP, city/state, or office name. Show local results, five sizes, availability, and location-specific prices for 3, 6, and 12 months. Require automatic renewal on 3-month terms and offer a choice on longer terms. Capture applicant name, email, mailing address and terms acceptance. Review the office address, size, term and synthetic total with a clearly labeled account payment option. On confirmation append a `poBoxes` record with `status: reserved`, `officeId`, size, term, applicant/contact, auto-renew preference, and creation metadata. Decrement the selected `postOffices[].inventory[size].available` by one and append a linked `poBoxTransactions` reservation in the same save. Show the confirmation code and office visit instructions. Reserved records need no assigned box number.
- [x] **PO Boxes — renew an active rental.** Show linked office, box number, expiration, previous transactions, and a Renew action. Review the selected term, amount, next expiration and auto-renew. Extend from the later of the existing `expiresOn` or today, adding calendar months and clamping the day to the destination month's end. Update the existing `poBoxes` record's `expiresOn`, `termMonths`, `autoRenew`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy`. Append one linked renewal to `poBoxTransactions` with the amount, prior/next expiration and term in the same save. Use catalog pricing and keep inventory stable during renewals.

## Scope boundaries

The account opens already signed in. Keep checkout, evidence, confirmations and contact details local and synthetic. Domestic claim coverage is limited to the four services defined in the data model. Model scheduling in calendar dates, without carrier cutoff times or a postal holiday calendar. Treat the services independently. PO Box renewal is available for an active record with a known catalog price. Exclude access credentials, identity checks, actual payment collection, physical fulfillment, appeal handling, waitlists and profile editing.
