Mounting, viewing, filtering, validation errors, abandoned drafts, refreshes of cached sessions, and idle tabs produce no action writes. Use the browser's clock for new timestamps and relative date logic. Keep `/go` state JSON-serializable and document the implemented keys and exact writes in root `SCHEMA.md`.

## P1 — Seed workflows

- [x] **My Trips.** Show Upcoming, Past and Canceled lists with destination, date, confirmation code, route and trip status. Filter by destination, airport code or confirmation, sort by departure, and open an itinerary with its segments, travelers, fare and ticket details. A Find a trip form accepts confirmation or ticket number and the traveler's first and last name, normalized for case and whitespace. Show the match and enable **Add to My Trips** for a matched unlinked trip. Explain missing results and link to details for an already linked trip. On confirmation write `trips[].accountId` and `trips[].linkedAt` and append a linked `tripActions[]` record. List/detail reads and lookup alone leave state unchanged.
- [x] **Change flight.** On an eligible, upcoming trip, use Change flight to choose a segment and new date, select a matching synthetic inventory option, and review old/new flight, fare difference, fee, and net amount due or credit before confirming. Provide actionable empty results and return-to-trip navigation. Confirm by updating only the selected `trips[].segments[]` entry, `fareAmount` and `updatedAt`, then append `tripActions[]` with old/new segment snapshots and the full settlement breakdown. Append an `eCredits[]` record for a positive residual. Revalidate the latest trip and inventory at confirm and reject stale review or duplicate confirmation.
- [x] **Cancel trip.** From eligible upcoming details, show the affected flights, ticket value, cancellation fee, and expected refund or eCredit on a review screen. On **Confirm cancellation**, set `trips[].status`, `canceledAt` and `updatedAt`, append `tripActions[]`, and append either an `eCredits[]` record or a `refunds[]` record when the settlement is positive. Show the resulting record reference, keep the itinerary readable under Canceled, and make repeated confirmation impossible. A zero-value cancellation still creates the cancellation action record.
- [x] **SkyMiles.** Show membership tier, miles balance, MQDs and recent activity. Filter activity by type and date and show the linked ticket when present. Add **Request mileage credit** for an eligible completed synthetic Delta ticket entered by 13-digit ticket number. Show inline errors for invalid, unmatched, already credited, or previously submitted tickets. Append a `mileageRequests[]` record with status `submitted` and the linked ticket and trip on confirmation. Display request history and its reference. Leave the mileage balance and activity ledger unchanged while the request is submitted.
- [x] **eCredits wallet.** Show available, expired and used credits with balances, source and expiration. Provide a lookup form for matching an unlinked synthetic eCredit by number and original ticket holder name. On explicit **Add to wallet**, set `eCredits[].accountId` and `linkedAt` and append an `eCreditActions[]` record with type `linked`. The same credit becomes selectable in the wallet. An unmatched or already linked credit leaves state unchanged.
- [x] **Redeem eCredit.** Select one available credit, find an eligible synthetic one-way flight by route and date, select a result, and review fare, amount applied, remaining credit, and payment due. Use fixed synthetic settlement without payment entry. On confirmation create a linked, confirmed `trips[]` record, decrement `eCredits[].remainingAmount`, update `status` and `updatedAt`, and append a completed `eCreditActions[]` redemption with the trip ID, allocation and amount paid. Permit partial use, prevent expired or exhausted credit use, and revalidate credit and offer before committing. The confirmation links to the new itinerary in My Trips.
- [x] **Workflow verification.** Build the app and use the installed Node Playwright from `PILOT_PLAYWRIGHT` with Chromium, on localhost:5323 only. Assert the corresponding `/go?sid=…` writes for linking trips, both change settlements, cancellation to credit and refund, mileage request, wallet linking, and partial and full redemption. Cover the zero-credit cancellation and negative paths, confirm that previews and idle tabs post nothing after the baseline, and verify sid isolation, reloads and injected empty, singleton and large arrays. Record exercised workflows, results and state fields in `TEST.md`. Keep scratch files gitignored or remove them. The parent runner handles Docker certification and commits.

## Data seed

- [x] In `src/data/seed.js`, export `createInitialData()` with deterministic fixtures and fixed IDs. Use 30 trips across current, historical and canceled travel, including lookup-only trips and an eligible completed ticket without mileage credit. Include 48 flight offers across the change and redemption scenarios, 36 mileage activity rows and 24 eCredits with available, expired, used and lookup-only cases. ## Out of scope

Authentication, account creation, airline network integrations, live payments, mileage transfers, award shopping, companion certificates and a general-purpose flight booking engine.
