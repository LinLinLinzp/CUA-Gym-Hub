## P1 — Primary workflows

- [x] **Find and read articles.** The workspace presents article entry points and a watchlist activity preview derived from state. Search matches title, topic and text case-insensitively and gives a clear empty state. The catalog supports topic filtering and title sorting. Article details show the rendered introduction, section contents, and discoverable `Article`, `Talk`, `Edit`, `History` and watch controls. Search, filters, reading and history inspection write no state.
- [x] **Edit an article and verify its revision.** Support full-page editing and section editing from an article. Prefill the selected text, keep drafts in the editor, and offer `Show preview`, `Show changes`, `Cancel` and `Publish changes`. Show a summary field, a `Minor edit` checkbox and a `Watch this page` checkbox reflecting current watch status. Disallow empty or unchanged edits, enforce a 500-character summary limit, and preserve other sections on a section publish. Show success and the published content on return. **Writes:** replace the edited `articles[].body`, `articles[].currentRevisionId` and `articles[].updatedAt`. Append `revisions[]` with `id`, `articleId`, `parentRevisionId`, `actorId`, `createdAt`, `status`, `body`, `summary`, `minor`, `section` and `byteDelta`. If watch status was changed, apply the watch mutation in the same save. History shows newest first with contributor, timestamp, summary, minor marker and byte delta. Selecting a revision opens the snapshot and shows changes against its available parent.
- [x] **Start a talk-page topic and reply.** Open `Talk` from any article. List topics by most recent activity and offer `Add topic` in both populated and empty views. The topic form requires a subject and first message. On submit, open the thread with the first signed message and persisted timestamp. `Reply` on each comment opens a composer for that comment. Reject whitespace-only input, keep drafts when validation fails, and clear them after successful submission or cancellation. **New topic writes:** append `talkTopics[]` with `id`, `articleId`, `subject`, `actorId`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `status` and one root `talkComments[]` record with `id`, `topicId`, `articleId`, `parentCommentId: null`, `actorId`, `body`, `createdAt`, `status`. **Reply writes:** append a `talkComments[]` record with the same fields and a real `parentCommentId`, and set the linked `talkTopics[].updatedAt` to the reply timestamp. Comment author and timestamp come from state. Render nesting with a depth cap for layout and retain access to the reply action on mobile.
- [x] **Watch and unwatch an article and its talk page.** Put `Watch` or `Unwatch` on both the article and talk views with an accessible pressed state, immediate feedback and the same state on either route. One active membership covers both views. **Watch writes:** append `watchlist[]` with `id`, `articleId`, `actorId`, `createdAt`, and `status: 'active'`, and append `watchlistEvents[]` with `id`, `watchId`, `articleId`, `actorId`, `createdAt`, `action: 'watch'`, `status: 'completed'`. **Unwatch writes:** remove that membership and append the same event shape with `action: 'unwatch'`. Reject duplicate/no-op watch actions without a save. Rewatch creates a new membership ID.
- [x] **Review and manage the watchlist.** In `Activity`, combine published article revisions and talk comments for currently watched articles, grouped by day, newest first. Show links to the article revision or talk thread, the contributor, time and summary or topic. Provide title search and change filters `All changes`, `Article edits` and `Talk comments`. In `Watched pages`, list memberships even when there is no activity. Provide search, row selection and a confirmed `Remove selected` action. Keep selections predictable when filtering by clearing them on filter changes. **Bulk removal writes:** remove all selected `watchlist[]` memberships and append one linked `watchlistEvents[]` unwatch record per removal, in one save. Activity reads and management filters write no state.
