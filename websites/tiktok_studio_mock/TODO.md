## P1 — SEED workflows

- [x] **Posts — find, publish, schedule, edit, and delete.** In Posts, show a thumbnail and caption, audience, status/date, and numeric engagement. Search caption and hashtags, filter status and audience, and sort newest/oldest or by views. Link each published row to its analytics. From New post, select an original sample clip or attach a local video using the Harbor media endpoint, enter a caption, choose a cover and audience, and set comment, Duet and Stitch permissions. Validate a nonblank caption and available media on publish/schedule. Offer Post now and Schedule, with a future local date/time converted to ISO. Show a schedule badge and date while keeping the record stable as time passes. Published rows support editing caption, cover, audience and permissions. Scheduled rows support cancellation through the confirmed delete action. Confirm deletion and remove the row from active views. **Writes**: create a `posts[]` record with the media/form fields and `status`, `publishedAt` or `scheduledAt`, zero metrics, actor and timestamps. Editing writes those editable fields plus `updatedById` and `updatedAt`. Deleting writes `status: 'deleted'`, `deletedAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedById` on the existing post.
- [x] **Drafts — save, reopen, edit, discard, and publish.** In the composer offer Save as draft, including partial work containing a caption or media. In Drafts, show previews or fallbacks, captions, last saved dates, and media readiness. Search captions, sort by last updated or oldest, and reopen the selected draft in the same composer with saved values. Save edits in place. Confirm discard and hide the row. Publishing or scheduling via the draft uses the standard post validation and transitions it out of the active draft list. **Writes**: append or update `drafts[]` with the composer fields, status, actor and timestamps. Discard sets `status: 'discarded'`, `discardedAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedById`. Publishing/scheduling creates a linked `posts[]` record with `sourceDraftId` and atomically sets the draft's `status: 'converted'`, `postId`, `convertedAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedById`.
- [x] **Comments — find, reply, heart, and moderate.** Show each audience comment's author, body, post context, timestamp, and reply/heart state. Search body or username, filter by post and by All, Unanswered or Replied, and sort newest/oldest. Keep filters useful with missing posts through a labeled fallback. Open an inline reply field, reject blank input, and show the submitted reply in its thread. Heart/unheart with a pressed state. Delete from the row menu after confirmation and exclude removed comments from the inbox. Replies to removed comments stay linked in persisted state. **Writes**: append a `comments[]` reply with `parentCommentId`, `postId`, creator actor, trimmed body, `status: 'visible'` and timestamps. Heart/unheart writes `likedByCreator`, `likedAt`, `likedById`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedById`. Confirmed deletion writes `status: 'deleted'`, `deletedAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedById` on the selected comment.
- [x] **Analytics — compare, drill down, and save/export.** Show a date range, metric tiles, a selectable trend chart, and published-post ranking. Offer Last 7 days, Last 28 days, Custom, and an explicit Latest available option for historic data. Overview shows post views, profile views, likes, comments, shares, and net followers. Post detail shows a scoped chart and a labeled lifetime metrics block, using per-post samples for the selected period. Dates, charts, summaries, and CSV all describe the same inclusive range. Invalid ranges get inline validation, and zero/absent data has a readable empty state. Provide Save report and Export CSV, plus saved reports with a way to re-open the saved selection. Saved totals stay fixed even if underlying data changes. **Writes**: append `analyticsReports[]` on Save report or successful Export CSV with `id`, creator `actorId`, `createdAt`, `scope`, `postId`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `metric`, `totals`, `sampleCount`, `format`, and `status` as specified in the data model. Range changes, chart inspection, opening a saved report, and viewing post detail are read-only.
