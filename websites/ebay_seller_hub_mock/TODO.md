## P1 — Required seller workflows

- [x] **Listings — browse, create, revise, end, relist.** Show counters and status filters for Active, Draft, and Ended, category filter, title/SKU/ID search, stable sorting, and pagination. Rows show local thumbnail, title/SKU, condition, price, quantity, status, and actions. Create and revise through the same editor with title, SKU, category, condition, description, positive price, and integer quantity. Support Save draft and Publish with stronger publish validation and unique SKUs per listing. End active listings after confirmation, including selected rows, and relist an ended listing with positive stock. **Writes:** create or update `listings[]` (`id`, editable fields, `status`, `updatedAt`, relevant lifecycle timestamps), append `activity[]` (`listing_created`, `listing_revised`, `listing_published`, `listing_ended`, `listing_relisted`), one entry per listing affected. Show the changed row or a link to its destination filter when the current tab excludes it.
- [x] **Orders — fulfill and cancel.** Filter by All, Awaiting shipment, Shipped, and Canceled. Search order number, buyer, item title or SKU, and sort by order date or total. Detail shows line items, shipping destination, totals, and fulfillment history. On awaiting shipment, support Mark as shipped with confirmation and Add tracking with carrier and tracking number. Saving tracking marks the order shipped. On shipped orders, support adding or editing tracking and preserve the original shipped timestamp. Allow canceling an awaiting-shipment order with a reason and a paid total review. **Writes:** update `orders[]` (`status`, `updatedAt`, `shippedAt`, `tracking`, `canceledAt`, or `cancellationReason` as applicable). Paid cancellation also sets `paymentStatus` to `refunded` and appends an issued `refunds[]` record with line-level quantities and totals. Append `activity[]` (`order_shipped`, `order_tracking_saved`, `order_canceled`), using the tracking event alone when tracking also ships the order.
- [x] **Returns — respond and resolve.** Expose a standalone queue linked from Orders and Overview. Filter by Needs response, Accepted, Received, Offer sent, and Closed. Search return ID, order number, buyer, or title. Detail shows requested lines, reason, amounts, linked order, and history. Accept a requested return with shipping responsibility and optional authorization number. Preferred test fixtures include accepted cases ready for a seller to confirm receipt and received cases ready for a refund. Support Mark received on accepted returns, Issue full refund on requested or received cases, and Offer partial refund once per requested case. Confirm amounts and prevent duplicate issuance or offers. **Writes:** update `returns[]` (`status`, `updatedAt`, and the action timestamp and related fields in the data model), append `refunds[]` with `issued` for full refunds and `offered` for partial offers, and append `activity[]` (`return_accepted`, `return_received`, `return_refunded`, `return_partial_offered`). If issued refunds cover the entire paid amount, update `orders[].paymentStatus` to `refunded` and set `orders[].updatedAt` in the same write. Sending an offer leaves the order intact. All refunds and offers link to the order, return, and original line IDs.
- [x] **Promotions — draft, schedule, launch, end.** Filter by status and type, search by promotion name or code, and sort by dates. Support Sale event, Order discount, and Coupon in a guided editor with details, inventory selection from eligible active listings, and date review. Require valid dates, a percentage or fixed amount, and inputs specific to the type. Order discounts require a minimum subtotal and coupons require a unique code. Save drafts, edit draft or scheduled offers, launch immediately or schedule, and confirm ending active or scheduled promotions. **Writes:** create or update `promotions[]` (`id`, `type`, `status`, `discount`, `listingIds`, `code`, `minimumOrderTotal`, dates and lifecycle timestamps), append `activity[]` (`promotion_created`, `promotion_revised`, `promotion_started`, `promotion_scheduled`, `promotion_ended`). Derive the effective status of published promotions from their saved dates and the runtime clock. No updates to listing base prices.
