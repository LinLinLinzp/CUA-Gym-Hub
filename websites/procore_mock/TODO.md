## P1 — Seed workflows

Each saved action writes one linked `activity[]` record alongside the business edits. See the data model for fields, actor attribution, and timestamps.

- [x] **RFIs — create, edit, and open.** Show Number, Subject, Status, Assignees, Due Date, and Location. Search number, subject, or question. Filter by status and assignee and sort by number or due date. Create a Draft with a subject and question, plus location, assignees, and due date as applicable. Allow editing Draft or Open RFIs. Saving or moving to Open requires an assignee and due date. **Writes** append `rfis[]` on create, update `subject, question, location, assigneeIds, dueDate` on edit, or update `status` on opening. Updates include `updatedAt, updatedById`.
- [x] **RFIs — respond and resolve.** Show the question, assignment, and reply history in detail. Add a text reply to an Open RFI, set or replace its official response, then close or reopen the RFI. Keep the official selection visible across status changes. **Writes** append `rfiResponses[]` with `rfiId, text, createdById, createdAt`; update `rfis.officialResponseId` on selection or `rfis.status` on close and reopen. Each operation updates RFI audit fields.
- [x] **Submittals — create, edit, and submit.** Show Number, Title, Spec Section, Revision, Status, Ball in Court, and Due Date. Search number, title, or spec section, filter by status and reviewer, and sort by due date. Capture title, type, spec section, description, responsible company, reviewer, and due date. Allow a Draft and a subsequent Submit for Review. Allow an optional file through the same-origin Harbor upload endpoint on save and expose saved attachments from the detail. **Writes** append `submittals[]` at revision 0, update editable fields and `attachments` on edit, and set `status = In Review` and `ballInCourtId = reviewerId` on submit. Edits and submission update audit fields.
- [x] **Submittals — review and revise.** On an In Review item, select Approved, Approved as Noted, Revise and Resubmit, or Rejected. Require comments for decisions other than Approved. Resubmit after Revise and Resubmit, keeping prior decisions visible with their original revision. **Writes** append `submittalReviews[]` with `submittalId, revision, decision, comments, createdById, createdAt`; update `submittals.status` to the decision and clear `ballInCourtId`. Resubmitting increments `revision`, sets `status = In Review` and restores `ballInCourtId = reviewerId`. Both actions update audit fields.
- [x] **Daily Log — record field activity.** Navigate dates with previous and next buttons, a date input, or a calendar. Show Weather, Manpower, Notes, and Deliveries. Add and edit typed entries and confirm entry removal. Allow entry on dates with no existing record. Derive crew-hours from worker count multiplied by hours per worker. **Writes** append `dailyLogs[]` on the first entry for a project and date, then append or edit `dailyLogEntries[]` with `dailyLogId, type, details` and audit fields. Deletion removes that entry and records its ID, type, and parent in the activity record. Each action updates the day's audit fields. Date browsing makes no state writes.
- [x] **Daily Log — complete and reopen.** Mark a day with entries Complete. Lock entry actions while complete. Reopen the day with its entries intact. **Writes** update `dailyLogs.status, completedAt, completedById, updatedAt, updatedById`; clear completion fields on reopen.
- [x] **Change Orders — price a change.** Show Number, Title, Contract, Status, Amount, and Schedule Impact. Search number, title, or contract, filter by contract and status, and sort by number or amount. Create a Draft under an eligible prime contract. Capture title, reason, description, requested reviewer, and signed schedule impact in days. Add, edit, or remove Schedule of Values rows with a cost code, description, quantity, and unit cost. Show line extensions and total. **Writes** append or update `changeOrders[]`; create or edit linked `changeOrderLineItems[]`, remove deleted lines, and update `changeOrders.totalAmount` in one save. Apply audit fields to the changed records.
- [x] **Change Orders — submit and decide.** Submit a Draft with a reviewer and at least one valid cost line. Approve or reject a Pending Approval order and require a comment for rejection. Show the pricing and decision in detail and the current status in the list. **Writes** update `changeOrders.status, submittedAt, submittedById` on submit; update `status, decisionComment, decidedAt, decidedById` on approval or rejection. Both update audit fields.
