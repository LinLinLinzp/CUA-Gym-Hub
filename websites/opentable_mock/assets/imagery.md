# Food imagery

Eight original WebP crops are bundled in `public/images/`. They were generated using the built-in imagegen tool as one contact sheet and cropped locally to its eight cells.

> Photorealistic editorial restaurant photography, one rectangular contact sheet of exactly eight independent photos in a clean 2-column by 4-row grid, straight flush boundaries, equal size tiles, no gutters. Each tile is a separate wide food photo, filling its whole cell, warm natural lighting, realistic restaurant plating, original imagery. Reading order: charred steak with carrots and green herbs on dark plate on dark wood, Italian pappardelle with ragu on cream pottery, Japanese nigiri on charcoal slab, French roast chicken and potatoes on blue-rim pottery, mezze with grilled lamb and hummus on linen, Mexican tacos on terracotta, Korean bibimbap, Cantonese dumplings. Entire dish mostly within center of each tile. No text, logos, borders, or collage elements overlapping tiles.
