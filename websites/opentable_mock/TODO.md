## P1 — Diner workflows

- [x] **Reservations — view and find.** Start on Upcoming with the nearest confirmed future booking featured, then show the remaining bookings in date order. Past shows completed visits and confirmed bookings whose scheduled time passed, with actions governed by their actual status. Canceled shows retained cancellations. Search these lists by restaurant or confirmation code and preserve the current tab when clearing a search. Detail shows status, restaurant, local date and time, party size, seating, confirmation code and any occasion or request. Use `new Date()` for temporal comparisons. Search, filters and opening details write no records.
- [x] **Reservations — book a table.** From Find a table, search by restaurant name or neighborhood, choose date, preferred time, party size and cuisine, then choose an actual available time at a restaurant. Show the selected table and booking summary, optional occasion and special request, and any prospective points. Confirm once and reveal the confirmation code and reservation link. Block past or unavailable times, invalid party sizes and conflicting bookings with inline feedback. **Writes:** append `reservations[]` with restaurant, actor, booking details, `status: confirmed`, confirmation code, `potentialPoints`, and timestamps. Append one linked `reservationEvents[]` record with `type: booked`, `before: null` and the resulting snapshot. Credits stay out of the posted points ledger until a visit is honored.
- [x] **Modify a reservation.** On future confirmed reservation details, open Modify with the current date, time, party size, seating, occasion and request prefilled. Recompute available times for the selected date and party, excluding the booking being edited from conflict checks. Preserve selection where still valid. Save updates in place, preserving reservation ID and confirmation code. No-change submissions and dismissed forms do not write state. **Writes:** update changed booking fields and `updatedAt` in the matching `reservations[]` record, including `potentialPoints` if the slot changes its eligibility. Append a linked `reservationEvents[]` record with `type: modified` and before/after snapshots.
- [x] **Cancel a reservation.** Provide Cancel reservation on future confirmed details. Open a confirmation dialog that names the restaurant and summarizes the date and party. Keep reservation dismisses the dialog. The explicit cancellation action moves the row to Canceled, preserves its detail URL and enforces the final status on repeated submissions. **Writes:** set the matching reservation `status: canceled`, `canceledAt`, `updatedAt`, and `potentialPoints: 0`. Append one linked `reservationEvents[]` record with `type: canceled` and before/after snapshots.
- [x] **Reviews — submit and read.** Show eligible completed, unreviewed visits under To review and submitted records under My reviews. Surface Write a review on eligible past reservation details as another entry point. Collect a required overall 1–5 rating, optional 1–5 food, service, ambience and value ratings, and an optional comment of up to 1,000 characters. Show named rating controls, an inline validation message for a missing overall rating and a submitted state in both views. Block duplicate reviews and submissions for canceled, upcoming or unconfirmed visits. **Writes:** append one `reviews[]` record with `reservationId`, `restaurantId`, `actorId`, ratings, trimmed comment, `status: submitted` and `createdAt`.
- [x] **Points — view balance and activity.** Show the spendable balance for the current diner, pending points on future confirmed bookings, signed transaction amounts, dates and the linked reservation or reward where available. Show a stable activity order and All, Earned and Redeemed filters. Seed posted earnings from honored visits. Booking, editing and cancellation update only prospective points through `reservations[]`; opening Points, changing activity filters or the passage of time adds no ledger records.
- [x] **Points — redeem for dining credit.** Show eligible dining credit options with point cost and USD value. Explain an insufficient balance beside the unavailable option. Confirm the selected option with its name, points cost and dollar amount, then show the resulting issued credit and reduced balance. Recheck affordability when confirming. Dismissing leaves the ledger unchanged and one confirmation creates one debit. **Writes:** append a `pointRedemptions[]` record with option, actor, points spent, dollar amount, currency, `status: issued` and `createdAt`; append a linked `pointTransactions[]` record with `type: redeemed`, negative `points`, `actorId`, `status: posted`, `redemptionId` and `createdAt` in the same store update.
