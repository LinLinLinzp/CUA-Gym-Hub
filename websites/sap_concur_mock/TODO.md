## P1 — Primary workflows

- [x] **Home and entry points.** Link **Create Report**, **Create Request**, and **Review Approvals** to their actual flows. Show recent documents with type, status and amount, and actionable counts for editable reports, editable requests and assigned pending approvals. Opening a row lands on its corresponding detail. Home and navigation write no state.
- [x] **Expense reports — library and headers.** List the active user's reports with ID, report name, purpose, updated date, total and status. Provide search by name, purpose or ID, status filter and stable sort by recency or total. The create form saves name, business purpose, report date, and cost center. Draft and sent-back details allow header edits and confirmed deletion. Creation appends `expenseReports[]` and an `activity[]` event; edits update the matching report and its `updatedAt` plus an event; deletion removes the report and nested lines, retains historical approval rows and events, and appends a deletion event.
- [x] **Expense reports — line items and receipts.** On an editable report, add, edit or remove lines with category, transaction date, merchant, amount, payment type and optional description. Show receipt state and allow attaching or removing a receipt through Harbor's same-origin upload or an original bundled receipt. Store metadata and the local URL on `expenseReports[].entries[].receipt`. Each accepted action writes the relevant `entries[]` record, recomputes `totalAmount` where amounts change, updates parent `updatedAt` and appends `activity[]`. Show entry count, total and any receipt requirement before submission. Keep drafts intact on validation or upload error.
- [x] **Expense reports — submit, recall and resubmit.** Confirm submission from the report detail. Require complete header and at least one valid line, enforce the exercise's receipt rule, and display assigned reviewer and amount. Submit writes `expenseReports[].status/approverId/submittedAt/updatedAt`, one new linked pending `approvals[]` record, and a submission event in `activity[]`. Owner recall writes parent `status/updatedAt`, changes the open `approvals[].status/decidedAt/decidedBy`, and appends an event. Returned reports show the reviewer comment and permit correction and resubmission. Each resubmission opens a fresh approval and retains previous decisions.
- [x] **Travel requests — library and itinerary.** List own requests with ID, name, destination, trip dates, estimated total and status. Search by name, purpose, destination or ID, filter status and sort by recent update, departure or estimated total. Create and edit with request name, business purpose, origin, destination, start/end dates and cost center. Confirm deletion for editable states. Write a `travelRequests[]` record on creation, update header fields and `updatedAt` on edit, or remove the parent and nested lines on deletion. Each mutation appends `activity[]` and leaves historical decisions accessible by snapshot.
- [x] **Travel requests — estimates and submission lifecycle.** Add, edit or remove expected Airfare, Lodging, Ground Transport, Meals or other expense lines with description, quantity and unit amount. Store `travelRequests[].expectedExpenses[]`, update `estimatedTotal/updatedAt`, and append `activity[]`. Submit a valid nonempty request to its assigned reviewer, writing parent `status/approverId/submittedAt/updatedAt`, a pending `approvals[]` record, and an event. Support owner recall while pending and correction/resubmission after send-back. Recall restores the parent to draft and updates its `updatedAt`, writes `approvals[].status/decidedAt/decidedBy`, and appends an event. Keep prior decisions intact on resubmission.
- [x] **Approvals — inbox, details and history.** Show Pending and History views for the active reviewer with type, title, employee, submitted date, amount and status. Filter by document type, search title, employee or ID, and sort dates/amounts. Open a detail with document header, lines, amounts, receipt links where relevant, and activity. Show a snapshot for historical approvals whose document was deleted. Pending counts come from assigned actionable approvals. Viewing, search, filters and sorting write no state.
- [x] **Approvals — approve and send back for both document types.** For an assigned pending approval, confirm **Approve** or **Send Back**; require a nonblank send-back reason and allow an optional approval note. In one save, update `approvals[].status/comment/decidedAt/decidedBy` and the linked `expenseReports[]` or `travelRequests[]` record's `status/updatedAt`. Append `activity[]` with actor, timestamp, approval ID, document link, decision, reason and amount. A processed item leaves Pending and appears in History. The owner sees matching status and comments. Prevent self-approval, duplicate open cycles, decisions on closed cycles and mutations on read-only documents.
