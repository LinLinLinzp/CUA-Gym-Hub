# Deel workflow research

Deel supports global workforce operations. This account centers on three jobs for a signed-in organization operator overseeing a boutique design studio. Jordan Lee manages contractor agreements, releases invoice payments and reviews team leave. Contractors and employees appear as synthetic people in linked records. Jordan can submit leave on behalf of employees in the managed team. The public product material informs business terminology and sequence. The interface design and account data are original.

## Views and data

- **Overview** pairs a work queue with accounts and leave summaries. Group financial amounts by currency and make queue items actionable links to the relevant list.
- **Contracts** opens a dense, searchable table and a grouped contract detail that links to invoice records. New and edit forms use a full page with the same header and review step. Drafts preserve the entered payment terms and selected worker.
- **Invoices** opens a filterable billing register with selection controls. Row detail itemizes charges and links back to Contracts. The payment review presents scope and totals before the confirmation writes the batch.
- **Time off** groups request history, balances and upcoming approved leave. The detailed review combines the worker's request and balance with the two decision controls. Request and edit forms use calendar dates, weekday quantity and a worker's own allowance.
- **Harbor state** contains staff, entities, agreements, invoices, payments, leave and linked activity. The data definitions identify keys, references, status values and exact writes. [Data model](data_model.md)

Account authentication, external communications, actual payment, payroll, full jurisdictional leave rules and contract template imports are beyond the local scenario. Client-side invoice CSV and the weekday leave policy are implementation choices for this workspace. [Original design specification](../DESIGN.md)
