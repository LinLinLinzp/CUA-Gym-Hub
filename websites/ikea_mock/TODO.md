## P1 — Required workflows

- [x] **Cart.** In Products, let users search product name or article number, filter by category and add an available product. In Cart, show each product, unit price, line total, labeled quantity control, remove action and subtotal. Adding a matching product increases its quantity, and an explicit removal clears the line. Enforce positive whole quantities and available stock when supplied. On each accepted edit, upsert or remove `cart.lines[]`, update `cart.updatedAt` and append `cartEvents[]` with operation, product/line IDs, before and after quantities, unit price and resulting line amount. Show a Browse products action if empty. Test adding, merging, changing quantity, removing and reloading.
- [x] **Delivery booking at checkout.** From a nonempty cart, show delivery address and ZIP, available time windows and an order review with merchandise, delivery, optional assembly and tax amounts. Confirm creates an `orders[]` record with line snapshots and totals, appends a scheduled delivery `bookings[]` record linked to that order and its lines, clears `cart.lines[]`, updates `cart.updatedAt` and appends a checkout `cartEvents[]` record with the consumed lines and order ID. Save those changes together. Show the confirmation and links to order and service details. Invalid ZIP, missing required address fields, expired/missing slot or invalid cart prevents submission without writes.
- [x] **Assembly booking.** At checkout, offer assembly for eligible line items with quantities, price, consent and a slot after the delivery window ends. Confirmation adds a separate `bookings[]` assembly record linked to the order, delivery booking and selected order lines in the same save as checkout. On an eligible existing order, offer Book assembly from order detail or Services and append only an assembly booking on confirmation. Use the order address and reject quantities already covered by scheduled or completed bookings, a slot before delivery or missing consent. Show the booked service, date/time, amount and included items on reload.
- [x] **Manage delivery and assembly.** In Services, show upcoming and past appointments with type, status, time and order reference. Allow rescheduling and cancellation for eligible scheduled bookings after explicit confirmation. Update the relevant `bookings[]` entry's `slot` or `status`, set `updatedAt` and append `history[]` with actor, timestamp and previous/next schedule or status. A delivery may not move past a scheduled assembly or be cancelled while one depends on it. Provide a direct link to that assembly when the dependency blocks the action.
- [x] **Returns.** Start from an order or the Returns hub. Show delivered or collected orders with available quantities. Let the user choose one or more lines and quantities, a reason and condition per line, and an eligible in-store or parcel method. Show merchandise refund estimate, fee and net amount before submission. On confirmation append a `returns[]` request with status `requested`, order ID, actor, timestamps, method, selected order/product IDs, quantities, reasons, conditions and monetary totals. Preserve the original purchase and derive remaining returnable quantity from active or completed requests. Show the reference and status on return detail and in the account history. Test partial and multi-line requests, reload, and blocked over-return attempts.
- [x] **Purchase and return history.** Show newest order first with reference, date, status and total. Support reference/item search and status filters. Order details show placed items, totals, and eligible service and return actions. Show return references, order links, date, estimate and status in Returns. History, search and view-only interactions write no records.
