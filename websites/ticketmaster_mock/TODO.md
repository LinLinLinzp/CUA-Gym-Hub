## P1 — Required account workflows

- [x] **Orders.** Open Orders by default. Show searchable event, venue, city and order-number metadata, with Upcoming, Past, All and Saved views and stable date sorting. Open a specific order to inspect seat labels, event notices, ticket status, purchase totals and itemized receipt. Include a deliberate **Save order** / **Remove from saved** control for repeat access. Save writes or reactivates an `orderSaves` record with its order id, actor, status, quantity and timestamp. Removing updates that record's status and audit fields. The saved list and button reflect the change after reload.
- [x] **Transfers.** From a transferable order, select one or more available seats, enter recipient name and email, review the selected seats and confirm. Write one pending `transfers` record and set selected `tickets` to `transfer_pending`. Show pending, accepted and canceled records in Transfers, with a deep link to the order. Confirm cancellation of an outgoing pending transfer to retain the record as canceled and release only its seats. Accept a seeded incoming pending transfer in Transfers to mark it accepted and release its reserved seats into the linked order. Keep outgoing accepted transfers as history. Record the actor, timestamp, seat ids and quantity for every transition.
- [x] **Resale.** From an eligible order, select available seats, enter a valid USD price per ticket, select a seeded payout method, and review the price, quantity and total before confirming. Create an active `listings` record and set the selected `tickets` to `listed`. Show Active, Sold and Removed in My Listings. Permit price edits on active listings and persist the revised price, total and audit fields. Confirm removal to preserve the listing as removed and return only its seats to available. Show sold listings as history with their existing values.
- [x] **Refunds.** Show organizer-authorized refunds from eligible order details, with the policy and original payment summary. Review the full eligible order and itemized refund amount, select a reason, and confirm. Append a `refunds` record with its order and seat ids, reason, original payment reference, itemized totals, quantity, actor and timestamp. Set the order and its included tickets to `refund_requested`. Show requested and historical processing or completed records in Refunds. Display a reason or status in the order view for unavailable, automatic, expired and already-requested cases. Reject a duplicate submission and keep receipt totals intact.
- [x] **Eligibility and recovery.** Show action availability per order and per ticket. Prevent selecting a seat held by another workflow. Release seats only when a transfer or listing being closed still owns their hold. Canceling a review dialog, entering invalid data, following a deep link, and opening a receipt leave business state untouched. Show inline validation, preserve a valid form on correction, and report successful confirmation in context.
- [x] **Playwright acceptance on localhost.** Run build and test at port 5337 using the installed Playwright resolved from `PILOT_PLAYWRIGHT`. Verify save/remove order, send/cancel/accept transfer, create/edit/remove listing and request refund in the UI. Inspect each action's exact fields through `/go?sid=…`, with fresh SIDs for independent cases. Cover invalid, dismissed and duplicate submissions, blocked seats, at least one empty and one partial injected state, large lists, direct routes with reload, SID isolation, and an idle tab posting nothing after baseline. Record the workflow evidence and observed fields in `TEST.md` for the parent runner.
