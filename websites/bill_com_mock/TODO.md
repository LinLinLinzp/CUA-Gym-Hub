## P1 — Seed workflows

- [x] **P1-1 · Payables — find and review.** Show All bills, Drafts, For approval, Ready to pay, and Payment activity views. Table columns are Vendor, Invoice #, Due date, Approval, Payment, and Amount. Search vendor, invoice number and memo, filter by vendor and due date, sort by due date and amount, and paginate at 15 per page. A bill row opens its detail with both statuses, line items, total, vendor link, and activity. These actions are read-only.
- [x] **P1-2 · Payables — create and edit.** From New bill, choose a vendor and enter invoice number, invoice date, due date, description, and one or more line items with quantity and unit price. Show computed amounts and validate required fields, valid dates, positive totals, and case-insensitive invoice-number uniqueness within a vendor. Save draft or save changes on a draft or denied bill. **Writes** a new or updated `bills` record with `vendorId`, invoice fields, line items and amounts, both statuses, and actor/timestamps, plus one `activity` record (`bill_created` or `bill_updated`). A denied bill stays denied until resubmitted. Abandoned or invalid forms write nothing.
- [x] **P1-3 · Approvals — submit and resubmit.** From a draft or denied bill, select an approver and submit. The creation form also offers Save and send for approval, using one store transaction for creation and submission. **Writes** `bills.approvalStatus = 'pending'`, `bills.approvalId`, and bill update metadata, appends a pending `approvals` record with the bill and approver IDs plus submission actor/time, and appends `activity` (`approval_submitted`). Preserve earlier denied approval records and their reasons.
- [x] **P1-4 · Approvals — approve or deny.** Show actionable rows assigned to the active user and a decision-history view. Open the bill to review amount, dates, line items, and past decisions. Approve with an optional note or deny with a required reason. **Writes** approved/denied status to the linked `approvals` record and `bills.approvalStatus`, writes `decisionNote`, `decidedBy`, `decidedAt`, and bill update metadata, and appends `activity` (`approval_approved` or `approval_denied`). Bills are payable only with approved status. A duplicate decision or a missing assignment writes nothing.
- [x] **P1-5 · Vendor payments — choose a vendor and bill.** Search the Vendors directory by name or email and filter by payment method. Vendor detail shows contact info, related bills, payment history, and a Pay bills action. Offer a payment entry point here, from the Payments page, and on an approved unpaid bill. The payment form preselects a bill when entered from its detail. Choosing a vendor or bill writes nothing.
- [x] **P1-6 · Vendor payments — schedule.** Select an approved unpaid bill, ACH or check from that vendor's configured methods, a funding account, a process date of today or later, and an optional memo. Show the full amount due and a review step before confirming. Recheck eligibility and date at confirmation. **Writes** a `payments` record with bill/vendor links, method, funding account, process date, amount, memo, `status = 'scheduled'`, actor and timestamp, sets `bills.paymentStatus = 'scheduled'` with update metadata, and appends `activity` (`payment_scheduled`). Keep the bill out of Ready to pay while scheduled. Success links to the payment detail. A repeated confirmation writes no duplicate record.
- [x] **P1-7 · Vendor payments — track and cancel.** Show Payments table with vendor, invoice #, process date, amount, method, and status. Search by vendor/invoice/payment reference and filter by status or method. Payment detail shows links to vendor and bill plus funding label and activity. For scheduled items offer a Cancel payment confirmation. **Writes** `payments.status = 'cancelled'`, `cancelledAt`, and `cancelledBy`, restores `bills.paymentStatus = 'unpaid'` with update metadata, and appends `activity` (`payment_cancelled`). Preserve the cancelled payment and bill approval. Processing and paid items remain read-only. Dates passing have no effect on records.
