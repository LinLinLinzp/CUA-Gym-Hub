## P1 — Primary workflows

- [x] **Shop and find products.** Show category shortcuts, featured products and local store availability on the home page. Search names, brands, keywords and TCIN. Provide category, brand, price and current-store pickup filters, plus relevance, price and top-rated sorts. Keep search and filters in the URL. Show product tiles with thumbnail, title, price, rating, pickup availability and an eligible quick-add action. Product details show variant, quantity, shipping and pickup availability and a short shipping and returns section. **Writes** through Cart or Store selection actions below.
- [x] **Store selection.** Open My store from the header, product fulfillment or cart. Search locally by city, ZIP or store name. Selecting a store updates future pickup choices. Existing pickup lines retain their store until the guest explicitly changes them. If the injected store is missing, ask the guest to choose a store before pickup. **Writes** `guest.preferredStoreId`, `guest.updatedAt` and a `store_select` record in `cartEvents[]`.
- [x] **Cart: add, change quantity, change fulfillment and remove.** Confirm add with item, quantity and a View cart action. Merge identical product, variant and fulfillment keys. Group Cart by Shipping and Order Pickup store. Rows show item, price, quantity, fulfillment, store and Remove. On a method or store change validate the new choice before moving the line. Recompute the quantity badge and estimated summary immediately. Preserve invalid or unavailable rows so guests can remove them or choose another fulfillment. **Writes** `cart.items[]`, `cart.updatedAt` and `cartEvents[]` records with `action=add|quantity_change|fulfillment_change|remove`, affected line IDs, product, variant, method, store, quantities and amounts before and after, actor and timestamp.
- [x] **Cart: Save for later.** Move the selected line below the active cart and exclude it from the badge, totals and checkout. Move to cart restores the line or merges with a matching line after revalidation. Remove works from either list. **Writes** `cart.items[]`, `cart.savedItems[]`, `cart.updatedAt` and `cartEvents[]` with `action=save_for_later|move_to_cart|remove` and the affected line snapshots.
- [x] **Circle offers: browse, save and activate.** Show Deals, Bonuses and View saved at `/circle/dashboard`. Filter by category and search offer text. Show eligibility, minimum spend, applicable fulfillment and expiration beside the benefit. Most deals are automatic. Manual bonuses and coupons expose Save, Activate or Apply, and Remove as appropriate. Expose the matching offer and savings from product details and cart. **Writes** `circleOfferStates[]` per guest and offer, with `status=saved|active|removed`, the applicable timestamps and actor. Browsing and displaying a projected discount are read-only.
- [x] **Circle offers: redeem at checkout.** Price automatic deals and eligible activated coupons in cart and checkout using the same calculation. Show ineligible or expired offers without a discount. Show a qualifying activated bonus as a Circle reward earned on order placement. **Writes on Place order** `circleRedemptions[]` with linked order and offer IDs and discount or reward cents, updates applicable manual rows to `circleOfferStates[].status=redeemed` and `redeemedAt`, and records discounts and offer IDs on `orders[]` and `orders[].items[]`.
- [x] **Order Pickup: checkout and place an order.** Require pickup stock at the chosen store and show store address, availability, masked payment, totals and optional alternate pickup person. Validate full name and email as a pair. Checkout also supports seeded standard shipping and distinct review sections for mixed carts. Disable submission for empty, invalid or stock-conflicting carts. Place the order once, show its order number and link to Purchase history. **Writes atomically** `orders[]` with line, guest and money snapshots, `pickupOrders[]` with store, alternate person, status `preparing`, pickup line IDs and a `placed` event, clears purchased `cart.items[]`, updates `cart.updatedAt`, appends a `checkout` event to `cartEvents[]` and writes applicable Circle records.
- [x] **Order Pickup: find orders and manage a ready pickup.** Purchase history supports order number or item search and status filters. Order Details shows Preparing, Ready for pickup, Picked up, Canceled or Pickup window ended. A ready record shows pickup deadline, store instructions and a View barcode dialog. For eligible records support Edit pickup person, one 3-day Extend pickup and Cancel items with a review step. Ready, collected and already expired cases come from fixtures or injected state. **Writes** person edits to `pickupOrders[].alternatePickupPerson`, extensions to `holdUntil` and `extensionCount`, cancellations to `pickupOrders[].status` when all its lines are canceled and `orders[].items[].status` for selected lines plus parent `orders[].status` and relevant `updatedAt` fields. Each mutation appends a `pickupOrders[].events[]` record. Canceling captured ready lines also appends a `refunds[]` record with `source=pickup_cancellation`. Opening the barcode and projecting expiration are read-only.
- [x] **Returns: start a return with selected quantities.** From eligible fulfilled Order Details choose Start a return. Select one or more lines and quantities, require a reason per selected line, and choose Return to store or Return by mail from the intersection of eligible methods. Display disabled lines and reasons for final sale, expired, unfulfilled or exhausted quantities. Review paid merchandise, tax, estimated refund and original payment before submission. Show the return number and an in-store barcode or printable local mail label. **Writes atomically** `returns[]` with item, quantity, reason, method, instructions and amounts, and linked `refunds[]` with `source=return`, exact line amounts, original payment snapshot and `status=pending`.
- [x] **Returns: track from Order Details.** Show submitted and completed requests, links to reopen instructions and refund totals and status per request. Recompute returnable quantity from active and completed requests. Seed a partial return, a mailed return and an issued refund so guests can test both new and in-progress cases. **Writes** through Start a return only. Viewing and printing existing instructions are read-only.

## Acceptance scenarios

Verify the state for each scenario with `GET /go?sid=<sid>` or `GET /state?sid=<sid>` after the save settles, then reload under the same `sid`. Use a fresh browser context and session ID when testing injected data.

| Seed workflow | Required Playwright actions | Verifier checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cart | Search for a product, add it, change its quantity and fulfillment, save it for later, restore it and remove it. | `cart.items`, `cart.savedItems`, `cart.updatedAt` and action-specific `cartEvents[]` reflect each step. The cart badge excludes saved items. |
| Circle offers | Save an offer, remove it, activate a qualifying manual offer, then place an order with it and a separate automatic deal. | `circleOfferStates[]` tracks save, remove, activation and redemption. `circleRedemptions[]` and `orders[].items[].offerDiscounts` link the applied savings to the order. An expired or ineligible offer contributes 0 cents. |
| Order Pickup | Change stores, place an in-stock pickup order with an alternate person, and open confirmation. Separately use a ready fixture to view the barcode, edit the person, extend and cancel items. | `guest.preferredStoreId`, `orders[]`, `pickupOrders[]`, linked events and captured cancellation refund are correct. Failed validation and barcode viewing cause no extra writes. |
| Returns | Open a collected or delivered order, submit a partial in-store return and reopen its barcode. Use a second eligible order to submit a mail return and open its printable instructions. Attempt to over-return a line. | `returns[].lines` preserve exact unit indexes, quantities, reasons and method. `refunds[].lines` and `amountCents` match the original paid amounts. Invalid repeats cause no write. |

Also verify empty, sparse and large injected states, two isolated sessions, a hard reload at each primary deep link, and one initial baseline save with no later idle posts. Cover both a successful and blocked path for each workflow.

Write or update `SCHEMA.md` from the implemented state, with every top-level key, each record shape and an **Observable State Changes** table of exact field paths. The Playwright agent records actions, evidence and fields changed in `TEST.md` and adds `WORKFLOWS: PASS` after all four seed workflows pass. Run `npm run build` and stage the built app at `factory/build/sites/target/dist` through the factory workflow. From the parent checkout containing `factory/`, run `python3 factory/hub_tools/dyn_certify.py target` and require a pass.
