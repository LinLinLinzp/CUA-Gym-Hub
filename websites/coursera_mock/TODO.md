## P1 — Seed workflows

- [x] **Enrollments — discover, enroll, withdraw and rejoin.** In Explore, search course title, partner and topic, filter by topic and level, and sort by title or duration. Course detail shows partner, syllabus, duration and certificate availability. An **Enroll** confirmation creates an `enrollments` row with `id`, `learnerId`, `courseId`, `status: "in_progress"`, `progressPercent: 0`, `createdAt` and `updatedAt`. Update the CTA to **Continue learning** and show the course in My Learning. My Learning provides status filters and a search across the learner's courses. Confirm **Unenroll** for an in-progress course, updating `status: "withdrawn"`, `withdrawnAt` and `updatedAt`; stop accepting work while withdrawn. **Re-enroll** updates that row back to `in_progress`, sets `reenrolledAt` and `updatedAt`, and clears `withdrawnAt`. Rejoining retains attempts and lesson progress. An active or completed enrollment blocks duplicate creation.
- [x] **Enrollments — track course progress.** From the course outline, open an original reading and choose **Mark complete**. Append a unique `lessonCompletions` row (`id`, `learnerId`, `enrollmentId`, `courseId`, `lessonId`, `status: "completed"`, `createdAt`) and update `enrollments.progressPercent` and `updatedAt` in the same save. The outline shows the next unmet requirement and reflects completed work on reload. Requirements and the progress formula are defined in the [state schema](assets/data_model.md).
- [x] **Assignments — find, submit, review and retake.** The Assignments desk lists coursework for active and completed enrollments, with course/status filters, due dates where present, next actions and grades. The course outline links to the same assignment page. Show quiz questions, pass threshold and prior attempts. Require an answer to every question and confirm **Submit quiz**. Grade deterministically from the seeded answer key and append an `assignmentAttempts` row with its IDs, `attemptNumber`, `answers`, `scorePercent`, `status: "passed" | "failed"` and `createdAt`. Recalculate progress using any passing attempt and update the enrollment timestamp for an accepted submission in the same save. Show score and outcome immediately and allow a failed quiz to be retaken with a fresh form and a new attempt row. Keep existing history and never count two passes as two requirements.
- [x] **Certificates — complete a course and earn the credential.** When all linked requirements are satisfied, show **Finish course** on the course outline. On confirmation set `enrollments.status: "completed"`, `progressPercent: 100`, `completedAt` and `updatedAt`. For a certificate-eligible course, add one `certificates` row in the same save. Include `id`, `learnerId`, `enrollmentId`, `courseId`, `courseTitle`, `learnerName`, `issuerName`, `verificationCode`, `status: "issued"` and `createdAt`. Offer **View certificate** after success and list it under Accomplishments. A course with no valid linked requirements cannot be finished. Repeated or stale confirmation must not duplicate issue.
- [x] **Certificates — find, download and copy a verification link.** In Accomplishments, search by course or issuer and sort by issue date. Show a locally rendered credential with recipient, issuer, issued date and verification code. **Download PDF** generates a readable, offline PDF with those values. **Copy verification link** copies a same-origin `/verify/:verificationCode` URL with the session ID where present. After a download is initiated or a link successfully copied, append a `certificateActions` row with `id`, `learnerId`, `certificateId`, `courseId`, `type: "download" | "copy_link"`, `status: "completed"` and `createdAt`. Record `fileName` for a download or `verificationPath` for a copy. Opening a certificate or its verification page is read-only. Surface clipboard failure with a selectable URL.
