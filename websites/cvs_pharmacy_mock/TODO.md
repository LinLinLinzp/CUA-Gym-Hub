## P1 — Required workflows

- [x] **P1.1 Prescriptions.** Search by medication or Rx number, filter by patient and visibility, and see medication, strength, last fill, refills remaining, and pharmacy in the list and detail. History supports patient/month/year filters and shows historic fills, quantities, and amounts paid. Provide “Hide prescription” with confirmation and “Restore prescription” from the Hidden filter. **Writes:** update only the selected `prescriptions[]` record’s `hidden`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy` on confirm. Listing, opening details, and filtering history write no records.
- [x] **P1.2 Refills.** Start from the Refills route or a prescription detail with that row preselected. Select one or more eligible prescriptions for one patient. Show readable reasons when a prescription is too early, hidden, inactive, expired, out of refills, or already in an open order. Choose a pharmacy and a preferred pickup window, then review patient, items, quantities, store, time, and estimated total. On confirmation, revalidate eligibility and show the new order reference and pickup details link. **Writes:** append one `refillOrders[]` record in `submitted` status with line items, total, actor, and pickup link; append one linked `pickupOrders[]` record in `preparing` status with the same patient, store, line snapshots, and pickup window. Save both in one commit. The open order blocks duplicate requests without consuming an authorized refill at request time.
- [x] **P1.3 Pickup.** Show preparing, ready, and past pickups with patient, pharmacy, order reference, requested pickup window, and status. The detail view shows the store address, line items, pickup instructions, and a pickup code when available. Only ready pickups expose “Check in for pickup”. Confirm the patient and location, and offer counter or drive-thru where supported. Show a confirmation and the selected method on the order detail. **Writes:** update the selected `pickupOrders[]` record to `checked_in` with `checkedInAt`, `checkedInBy`, `checkInMethod`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy`. Reject repeats or stale submissions. Seed ready pickups so check-in can be exercised without a background progression. Store staff completion is outside the account flow.
- [x] **P1.4 Vaccine booking.** From Vaccines, view upcoming and past visits or start scheduling. Choose a patient, one to three vaccine options, a location that offers them, then an available day/time. Review patient, vaccines, address, and time before confirming. Show a receipt with the confirmation code and details link. **Writes:** append a `vaccineAppointments[]` record with `patientId`, `vaccineIds`, `storeId`, `scheduledFor`, `status: "scheduled"`, `confirmationCode`, `createdAt`, and `createdBy`. Reject missing selections, taken store slots, or simultaneous appointments for the same patient.
- [x] **P1.5 Vaccine appointment changes.** Open an upcoming appointment by ID. Reschedule through the available-slot picker with the patient and vaccines carried forward, then review the new time and location. Cancel through a separate confirmation. **Writes:** reschedule on the same appointment ID and confirmation code, setting `storeId`, `scheduledFor`, `rescheduledAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy`. Cancellation sets `status: "cancelled"`, `cancelledAt`, `updatedAt`, and `updatedBy`. Keep past and cancelled records visible in history. Reject no-op reschedules and changes to past or cancelled appointments.
