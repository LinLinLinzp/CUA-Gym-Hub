## Scope

This workspace covers internal corporate cards, accounts payable, review queues, and policy controls. Authentication, live payments, bank credentials, card secrets, external uploads, notifications to third parties, OCR, and production integrations are outside this phase. The payment action records a synthetic payment outcome locally.
