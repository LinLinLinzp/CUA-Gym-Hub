# DoorDash account state model

Use `createInitialData()` in `src/data/seed.js` to return the following JSON-safe object. IDs are stable strings in the seed. User mutations generate new IDs once on confirmation, with `createdAt` or `updatedAt` from `new Date().toISOString()`. Amounts use integer USD cents. A question mark denotes an optional field. Examples are synthetic.

Receipt and group cart items share `{id: "line-1", menuItemId: "mi-rice", name: "Ginger Rice Bowl", quantity: 2, unitPriceCents: 1395}`. A group receipt item may also carry `participantId`. Receipt totals use `{subtotalCents: 2790, deliveryFeeCents: 299, serviceFeeCents: 199, taxCents: 0, tipCents: 400, totalCents: 3688}`. Group participants use `{id: "acct-maya", name: "Maya Chen", role: "host", joinedAt: "2026-11-12T18:30:00.000Z", items: []}`. Guests have role `guest` and their own IDs. Keep the active guest ID in the current UI session and use the guest ID as `updatedBy` for their submissions.

## Data relationships and write rules

- Merchant menus drive group cart edits. Reorders use the original receipt item snapshots and recalculate totals in review. Checkout and reorder retain receipt snapshots independent of future menu edits. Recheck eligibility and positive integer quantities at confirmation.
- Group checkout creates one ordinary order with `groupOrderId` while the group receives its `orderId`. Make both writes in one mutation and reject repeat checkout. Moving between host and guest within a shared session must show the latest committed cart. Preserve `sid` and the token in the copied URL. Reads of closed, placed, and canceled groups never reopen them.
- Refund quantities are constrained per order line by quantity purchased minus the sum on requested and issued refunds. The refundable amount for each line is quantity times paid unit price, with the request total equal to the line sum. Declined records free the quantity. Show `resolvedAt` as the issue date only for issued refunds.
- Paid membership access and upcoming charges are distinct. A confirmed plan change updates `pendingPlanId` and records the next-billing effective time. Pause and paid cancellation clear the next charge and retain `accessUntil`. Cancel clears a pending plan change. Resume or reactivate within paid access preserves the paid period and restores its next charge date. New or expired enrollment sets the selected plan, starts a paid period and creates one linked charge. Derive expired access from `new Date()` in the view. A date boundary produces no writes.
- Seed linked records with coherent dates and amounts. Use around 28 delivered orders so that some remain wholly refundable alongside 24 linked historical requests. Seed 24 groups with several open and closed carts plus placed and canceled history. Spread 24 monthly charges across two years and include 24 meaningful membership events, with the active membership matching the latest relevant history.
- Keep UI search, form drafts, selection, and feedback out of the persisted keys until a business action is confirmed. Preserve unrelated injected records on save. Array replacement and object merging follow the unchanged Harbor helper.
