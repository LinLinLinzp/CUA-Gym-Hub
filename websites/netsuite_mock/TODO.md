## P1 — Primary workflows

- [x] **P1.1 Transaction lists and queues.** Build Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, Item Fulfillments, Item Receipts, Vendor Bills, Inventory Adjustments, Inventory Transfers, and Customer Payments lists. Use a View selector and expandable Filters, with applicable date, status, party, location and document-number criteria. Show date, number, party, status, amount and applicable quantity/balance columns with sorting, paging and Edit/View links. Queues show only eligible orders and lead to the same approval or prefilled conversion as the record view. Keep selections and filtering outside Harbor state.
- [x] **P1.2 Sales orders — Enter, edit and maintain.** Transactions → Sales → Enter Sales Orders. Require customer, date, location and at least one valid item line. Default terms, tax, shipping and sales rep from the customer where available. Provide searchable customer/item pickers, Items with Add/OK/Remove, Shipping and Billing sections, and a live Summary. Put customer credit hold and credit-limit checks before Save and approval. On edit, preserve linked line IDs and protect quantities already fulfilled or billed. Offer confirmation for Cancel Order in Pending Approval, Close Order for eligible open orders and Delete only without linked transactions. **Writes** `salesOrders` create/edit with header, `lines[].{lineId,itemId,locationId,quantity,rate,amount,quantityFulfilled,quantityBilled,isClosed}`, and totals. Cancel/close set `status`, `cancelledAt` or `closedAt`, and `lines[].isClosed`. Delete removes the order and keeps its audit note. Creation advances `counters.salesOrder` and `counters.nextInternalId`. Every mutation appends `systemNotes`. See §7 W1.1–W1.2, W1.4–W1.5, W1.8.
- [x] **P1.3 Sales orders — Approve and fulfill.** On the record or from Approve Sales Orders, transition Pending Approval to the derived operational status. Fulfill from the order or Fulfill Orders queue. The Item Fulfillment form shows Created From, warehouse, eligible inventory lines, remaining quantity, a Fulfill checkbox, and quantity per selected line. Allow part fulfillment, enforce aggregate stock at each location and suppress service lines here. Save as Shipped by default. If Picked or Packed is chosen, allow subsequent Mark Packed or Mark Shipped as appropriate. **Writes** `salesOrders.status, approvedById, approvedAt` on approval. Fulfillment creates `itemFulfillments` with `createdFromId`, `status` and `lines[].{orderLineId,itemId,locationId,quantity}`, advances `counters.itemFulfillment` and `counters.nextInternalId`, decrements `items[].locations[].onHand` once, increments `salesOrders[].lines[].quantityFulfilled` and derives its `status`. Packing/shipping updates only the fulfillment `status` and applicable `shippedAt` plus audit fields. Append `systemNotes`. See §7 W1.3, W1.6–W1.7.
- [x] **P1.4 Purchase orders — Enter, edit and approve.** Transactions → Purchases → Enter Purchase Orders. Require vendor, date, warehouse and item lines. Include Receive By, employee, memo, terms, vendor reference, searchable items, quantity, rate, expected receipt date and Summary. Approve and Reject from the record or approval queue. Require a rejection reason. Guard edits below received/billed quantities, protect linked lines and prompt before closing or deleting child-free orders. **Writes** `purchaseOrders` create/edit with buying fields, totals and `lines[].{lineId,itemId,quantity,rate,amount,expectedReceiptDate,quantityReceived,quantityBilled,isClosed}`. Approval/rejection updates `status`, `supervisorApproval`, `approvedById/At` or `rejectedById/At` and `rejectionReason`. Close updates `status, closedAt, lines[].isClosed`; delete removes the PO. Creation advances `counters.purchaseOrder` and `counters.nextInternalId`. Append `systemNotes`. See §7 W2.1–W2.2, W2.5–W2.6.
- [x] **P1.5 Purchase orders — Receive and bill.** Receive from the PO or Receive Orders. Show Created From, warehouse, outstanding items, selected receipt lines and editable quantities capped by remaining quantity. Then Bill from the PO or purchasing billing queue with quantities capped by received minus billed, vendor invoice reference, terms, due date and Summary. **Writes** Receipt creates `itemReceipts` with source, warehouse, `lines[].{orderLineId,itemId,locationId,quantity,rate,amount}` and total, advances `counters.itemReceipt` and `counters.nextInternalId`, increments `purchaseOrders[].lines[].quantityReceived`, derives PO `status`, and for inventory items updates `items[].locations[].onHand, averageCost, lastPurchasePrice`. Bill creates `vendorBills` with source, `transactionNumber, tranId, vendorId, dueDate, lines, total, amountPaid, amountRemaining, status`, advances `counters.vendorBill` and `counters.nextInternalId`, increments `purchaseOrders[].lines[].quantityBilled` and derives PO `status`. Both append `systemNotes`. See §7 W2.3–W2.4.
- [x] **P1.6 Inventory — Item master and stock.** Lists → Accounting → Items. Filter by location, SKU/name, type and active status. Item view shows location on-hand, committed, available, backordered, on-order and reorder levels, with links to applicable transactions. Expose Current Inventory Status with item/location/low-stock filters. New Inventory Item and Edit provide SKU, display name, pricing, vendor, units, accounts, locations, reorder settings and inactive flag. Move existing stock through adjustment, transfer, receipt or fulfillment. **Writes** create/update `items` with SKU, type, prices, vendor/account links and `locations[].{locationId,onHand,reorderPoint,preferredStockLevel}`; create advances `counters.nextInternalId`. Edits update audit fields and append one `systemNotes` Change per changed field. Availability, reorder flags and stock views are derived. See §7 W3.3–W3.4.
- [x] **P1.7 Inventory — Adjust and transfer.** Transactions → Inventory → Adjust Inventory or Transfer Inventory. Adjustment requires a warehouse, adjustment account, date and a nonzero signed quantity per selected stock line. Show before/after quantity and estimated value. Transfer requires distinct From/To locations and positive quantities with source stock shown. Disallow insufficient inventory under the default preference, including combined quantities on duplicate item/location lines. **Writes** adjustment creates `inventoryAdjustments` with `accountId, adjustmentLocationId, lines[].{itemId,locationId,quantityOnHandBefore,adjustQtyBy,newQuantity,unitCost}, estimatedTotalValue` and updates `items[].locations[].onHand`. Transfer creates `inventoryTransfers` with `fromLocationId, toLocationId, lines[].{itemId,quantityOnHandFrom,quantity}` and subtracts/adds `items[].locations[].onHand` on the corresponding warehouses in one commit. Increment `counters.inventoryAdjustment` or `counters.inventoryTransfer` and `counters.nextInternalId`, then append `systemNotes`. See §7 W3.1–W3.2.
- [x] **P1.8 Invoices — Bill fulfilled goods and create standalone invoices.** From SO Bill or Bill Sales Orders, open a form prefilled with customer, terms, billing address and unbilled eligible quantities. Cap stock items at fulfilled minus billed and service/non-stock items at ordered minus billed. Also offer Transactions → Sales → Create Invoices. Show Items, Billing, due date, subtotal, tax, shipping and total. **Writes** create `invoices` with `createdFromId` (SO ID or null), `createdFromType`, `customerId, termsId, dueDate, lines[].{lineId,orderLineId,itemId,quantity,rate,amount}, subtotal, taxTotal, shippingCost, total, amountPaid, amountRemaining, status`. Only conversion updates `salesOrders[].lines[].quantityBilled` and derived SO `status`. Advance `counters.invoice` and `counters.nextInternalId`, then append `systemNotes`. See §7 W4.1–W4.2.
- [x] **P1.9 Invoices — Edit, void and accept payment.** List and record show Open, Paid In Full, Voided and a derived overdue flag. Offer edit and confirmed void for open unpaid invoices. Show related SO and payments. Accept Payment from the invoice or Transactions → Customers → Accept Customer Payments. Show the selected customer's open invoices, entered payment, explicit Apply checkboxes/amounts, method, account/Undep. Funds, applied total and unapplied amount. Validate per-invoice balance and aggregate payment. **Writes** edits update `invoices` fields, lines and amounts; void sets `invoices.status, amountRemaining, voidedAt, voidedById`. Linked edit/void adjusts `salesOrders[].lines[].quantityBilled` by net delta and derives SO `status`. Payment creates `customerPayments` with `customerId, payment, paymentMethodId, accountId, undepFunds, applied[].{invoiceId,amount}, appliedTotal, unapplied, status`, advances `counters.customerPayment` and `counters.nextInternalId` and updates each applied invoice's `amountPaid, amountRemaining, status`. Append `systemNotes`. See §7 W4.3–W4.5.
- [x] **P1.10 Customer and vendor records.** Lists → Relationships → Customers or Vendors. Show searchable lists with company, ID, contact/terms and balance. View related transactions. - [x] **P1.11 Home and global discovery.** Home shows operational reminder links to open approvals, fulfillable/receivable orders, overdue invoices and items to reorder.
