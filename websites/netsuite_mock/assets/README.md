# NetSuite workflows connect orders and stock

The NetSuite training workspace represents four daily ERP workflows for Cascade Industrial Supply. **Sales orders, purchase orders, inventory, and invoices are all P1 workflows** with linked business records and quantities a Harbor verifier can inspect. A fixed Administrator workspace supports sales, purchasing, warehouse, and accounts receivable tasks against the same industrial supply catalog. Partial fulfillment and receiving make the outstanding quantities visible across order, stock, and billing screens. Development runs on port **5447**. The canonical implementation checklist and field-level write sets live in the [TODO](../TODO.md), [data model](data_model.md), and [Harbor runtime contract](../../../HARBOR_CONTRACT.md).

## Four operators share the same transaction history

Sales operations enters orders for existing customers, confirms pricing and shipping terms, and routes orders for approval. Warehouse staff fulfills selected sales lines against location stock, then follows the linked fulfillment from the order. NetSuite's Advanced Shipping flow separates the fulfillment and invoice steps so the warehouse can ship part of an order and finance can bill eligible quantities. The sandbox reduces location stock when the fulfillment is first saved, including when the operator chooses Picked or Packed. A later packing or shipping action records the stage change. [Oracle sales order entry](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1216500.html) · [Oracle individual fulfillment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4576030995.html) · [Oracle Advanced Shipping](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1224089.html)

Purchasing enters a PO with vendor, destination warehouse, expected receipt dates, quantities and costs. The sandbox gives the supervisor one approval or rejection step. Receiving staff selects the delivered lines from the PO and enters actual quantities. With Advanced Receiving enabled, an Item Receipt records the receipt and updates inventory, and a later Vendor Bill records the amount owed for the received lines. The sandbox caps receipt at ordered quantity and uses an item-wide moving average for stock receipts. Warehouse staff can also post an adjustment for a count variance or make a direct transfer between two locations. [Oracle purchase order entry](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2400504.html) · [Oracle partial receipt](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2411754.html) · [Oracle billing after receipt](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2403862.html) · [Oracle inventory adjustments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161981111273.html) · [Oracle basic transfers](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4660882963.html)

Accounts receivable uses Bill Sales Orders for conversion and Create Invoices for manual billing. A converted invoice carries its source order and line IDs. Customer payments hold explicit allocations to one or more open invoices for the same customer, with separate applied and unapplied amounts. The sandbox treats standalone invoice entry as a receivables operation and uses service or non-stock examples in the seed. It permits voiding an unpaid invoice, preserves the historical line amounts, sets the balance to zero and reverses linked billed quantities. [Oracle sales order billing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1240951.html) · [Oracle invoice creation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1238506.html) · [Oracle payment application](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1288824.html) · [Oracle invoice voiding](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158654862294.html)

## The shell leads to lists, records and queues

The browser workspace uses a compact utility header and a vertical navigation rail. Transactions and Lists retain recognizable category and action names. Home shows reminder counts that link to filtered approvals, fulfillment, receiving, overdue invoices and replenishment work. Its KPI panel reflects the current workspace data. Global search recognizes document numbers, vendor references, SKU and party names, with keyboard-operable suggestions. Oracle documents role-specific menus and typed search prefixes, including `cu:` for customers and `invo:` for invoices. [Design guide](../DESIGN.md) · [Oracle navigation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4323290738.html) · [Oracle reminders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N581945.html) · [Oracle search prefixes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N639669.html) · [Oracle keyboard shortcuts](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N474926.html)

Transaction lists pair a View selector and applicable filters with a sortable, paged table of document numbers, parties, dates, statuses and balances or quantities. Action queues narrow the same records to eligible orders and open a prefilled conversion or approval. Record details expose status, related transactions and System Information. Editors group customer or vendor information, shipping and billing fields, totals, and item lines with searchable pickers. Fulfillment and receipt forms add a Created From link and editable quantity for selected lines. Inventory forms emphasize the warehouse, before/after stock and estimated value or destination. The payment form groups payment details with an Apply grid for invoice selection, remaining balance and applied amount. ## Linked lines make partial quantities verifiable

Customers and vendors carry terms and contact defaults. Orders link to the party and item master. The transaction documents carry stable IDs and the line items carry stable line IDs. Each fulfillment, receipt or converted bill has a source record ID and source line IDs. The verifier can therefore attribute each child quantity to the original line, even when an order contains repeated SKUs. A customer payment carries invoice IDs and applied amounts. Inventory adjustments carry before, change and after quantities. Transfers carry both location IDs and selected SKU quantities. Each successful save commits its records, counters and applicable audit notes together. [Data model, including observable state changes](data_model.md)

Item/location on-hand is a stored snapshot. Committed, available, backordered, on-order and reorder indicators are derived from that snapshot and open orders. Open balance and aging are derived from invoices and application history using the runtime clock. Seed IDs and quantities stay deterministic, with dozens of sales orders, POs, invoices and catalog items across Portland, Reno and Columbus. This supplies meaningful filter results and partial processing cases while letting Harbor replace arrays with injected task data. Authentication, external payment processing, external communications, advanced accounting, lot/serial/bin workflows, transfer orders and native mobile workflows are outside this plan. ## Playwright acceptance

Each acceptance scenario should cross a record boundary and inspect the affected balances. A partial receipt followed by a vendor bill distinguishes a stock write from an accounts payable write. A partially fulfilled order followed by invoicing tests that the sales order stays open for its remaining quantity. A rejected quantity or a cancelled form should leave the same state visible at `/go`. Record the linked IDs and exact changed fields in `TEST.md` when implementation is ready.
