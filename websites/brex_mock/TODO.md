- [x] **Expenses — submit and review.** **Submit reimbursement** collects merchant, positive amount with at most two decimal places, valid date, category, business memo and active spend limit. In pending detail, **Approve** or **Deny** with a required denial reason writes `expenses[].status`, `reviewedBy`, `reviewedAt`, `reviewReason`, `updatedBy` and `updatedAt`, plus an `expense.approved` or `expense.denied` event with amount, currency and decision.
