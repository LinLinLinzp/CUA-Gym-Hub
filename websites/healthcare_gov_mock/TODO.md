## P1 — SEED workflows

- [x] **Applications.** List application reference, year, location, status and last updated date. Provide text search by reference/name, filters for year/status, and newest-updated ordering with an empty state. **Start an application** chooses coverage year, state and ZIP; **Save and continue** creates a resumable draft. Collect contact, household members and who needs coverage, current monthly income and expected annual income. Use labeled steps with Back, Save and continue, and a review screen with edit links and an attestation. Verify required fields, ZIP and money values, and at least one household member seeking coverage. Zero income is valid. **Continue application** restores saved inputs and the last completed step. **Submit application** confirms the application reference and exposes **View eligibility results** and **Compare plans** when eligible. Prevent duplicate submissions from repeated clicks or reloads. **Writes:** draft creation appends `applications[]`; explicit draft saves update its section data, `lastCompletedStep`, `updatedAt` and `updatedBy`; submit updates its `status`, `submittedAt`, `updatedAt`, `updatedBy`, `latestEligibilityResultId` and `lastCompletedStep`, and appends `eligibilityResults[]` with `trigger: initial_application` in the same save.
- [x] **Plan selection.** Enter from eligibility or the account **Plans** view and make the application/year context visible. Search plan or insurer name, filter by metal category or network type, and sort by net monthly premium or deductible. Plan cards and details display gross/after-credit monthly premium, deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, network type and primary care copay. Compare two or three plans side by side with remove/clear controls. **Select plan** opens a review showing plan, covered members, effective date and monthly amounts. **Confirm plan selection** saves the plan and opens a receipt linked from the application. Use “Pending first premium” for a nonzero premium. Permit a later eligible application update to open shopping again at the new eligibility result. **Writes:** confirmation appends `planSelections[]` with the application, plan, eligibility, selected member IDs, status, date and captured pricing; updates `applications[].selectedPlanSelectionId`, `updatedAt`, `updatedBy`; and updates the prior current selection to `replaced` with `replacedAt` and `replacedBySelectionId`, when applicable. Review, compare and filtering leave `/go` unchanged. Reconfirming the current plan for the same result returns its existing receipt.
- [x] **Income updates.** Start with **Report a life change** on an already submitted application and choose **Income changed**. Show the previous monthly and annual amounts. Collect revised current monthly income, expected annual income, change date and income source, then show an old/new review and **Submit income update** action. Validate money and date inputs and require one amount to change. Leave persisted values untouched when backing out before confirmation. After confirmation, show the new eligibility result and a next step to review or compare plans as applicable. Keep the existing plan selection and its captured prices intact until an explicit selection. **Writes:** append `incomeUpdates[]` with old/new values, source, effective date, status and resulting eligibility ID; append `eligibilityResults[]` with `trigger: income_update` and the income update ID; update `applications[].income`, `latestEligibilityResultId`, `lastResubmittedAt`, `updatedAt` and `updatedBy`, together. Keep prior eligibility results readable and prevent repeat submission of the same revision.
- [x] **Workflow verification.** Use Node Playwright from `PILOT_PLAYWRIGHT` and Chromium against `http://localhost:5514` only. With a fresh SID, start and resume a draft after reload, submit it, compare plans and confirm one, report an income change and verify updated eligibility, then confirm a replacement from the new result.
