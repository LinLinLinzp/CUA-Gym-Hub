## P1 — Required identity and connected-account workflows

- [x] **Identity overview and history (SEED: identity).** Show the signed-in account, identity status, last verified date when present, and a `Verify identity` action for anyone without a current verification. If a draft already exists, expose `Resume verification`. Show attempt history with outcome and method filters, agency/service name search and most-recent-first ordering. Opening history, filtering, or reading the current status writes no state. Starting writes one `verificationAttempts[]` record with `status: 'in_progress'` and the account/actor IDs, timestamps and step.
- [x] **Complete or abandon synthetic verification (SEED: identity).** Implement a resumable flow with `Method`, `Review sample identity`, `Confirm sample contact`, and `Review and submit` screens. Select online or in person and use only visible sample data. Require an accepted type for the selected method and confirm the sample identity and contact before enabling submission. Each successful Continue saves the corresponding `verificationAttempts[]` fields and next step. Online confirmation updates the attempt to `verified` and writes `identity.status`, `verifiedAt`, `verificationAttemptId`, and `updatedAt` in the same save. In-person submission writes a `pending_in_person` attempt and shows a sample visit reference and deadline. A separate confirmed `Complete sample visit` action finishes it with the same identity and attempt writes as online. Confirmed cancellation updates only the active attempt to `canceled`. Leaving the screen keeps it resumable. Validation errors, Back, dialog dismissal, and duplicate submissions produce no writes.
- [x] **Find and inspect connected accounts (SEED: connected accounts).** Show `Your connected services` with Connected, Disconnected, and All filters. Search case-insensitively by service, agency, and resolved shared email. Default to latest update first with an A–Z sort option and an explicit clear-filters action. Detail pages show service, current status, shared fields, shared email, and timestamped consent activity. The catalog is searchable and indicates which services require verified identity. Listing, searching, sorting, and detail views write no records.
- [x] **Connect or reconnect an agency service with consent (SEED: connected accounts).** Offer `Connect a service` from the connected-services page and `Reconnect` on a disconnected record. Open a local consent screen summarizing the chosen service and requested attributes, with a choice of existing synthetic email addresses. A service requiring verified identity links to the local verification flow if needed, then returns to consent. Explicit `Agree and connect` creates or reactivates one `connections[]` record and appends one `connectionEvents[]` record of type `connected` or `reconnected` with after-state, actor, and related IDs. Successful verification alone writes no connection. Cancel or repeat an already-applied submission without writing another record.
- [x] **Change a shared email (SEED: connected accounts).** On an active connection, open `Change shared email` with current and alternative addresses. Require a different eligible address and confirm. Update only that connection's `sharedEmailId` and `updatedAt`, and append a `shared_email_changed` event with before/after email IDs and snapshots. Account email choices remain available to every connection. Cancel, missing eligible addresses, or choosing the current value produce no writes.
- [x] **Disconnect an agency service (SEED: connected accounts).** On an active connection, require confirmation naming the service. Set the connection to `disconnected` with `disconnectedAt` and `updatedAt`, and append a `disconnected` event with before/after status and actor. Keep the connection record visible under Disconnected, with its history and a Reconnect action where the service is available. Cancel or repeat an already-applied disconnect without writing.
- [x] **Browser acceptance and TEST.md.** Use installed Playwright from `PILOT_PLAYWRIGHT` and Chromium against `http://localhost:5544`. Exercise identity completion after reload, in-person pending/completion, and cancellation. Exercise connect, email change, disconnect, and reconnect, plus the verification-required gate and cancel/no-op paths. Check exact changes in `/go?sid=...` and preserved state after hard reload, no idle writes after bootstrap, isolated SIDs, and empty/single/large injected fixtures. Cover keyboard actions and desktop/mobile layouts. Record commands, results, and changed fields for both SEED workflows in `TEST.md`. The parent runner handles Docker certification and commits.
