## P1 — Required account workflows

- [x] **Orders.** Show order number, purchase date, status, fulfillment method, total, and item previews. Search by order number, product name, or SKU. Provide status, year, and fulfillment filters with a clear action and a no-results state. Open details with line items, totals, receipt view, and a local tracking or pickup panel for applicable orders. A ready-for-pickup order offers **Change pickup person** with name, email, phone, and a review step. A processing shipment or ready pickup offers **Cancel items**, with line selection, quantity, reason, and a confirmation showing estimated refund. Derive eligibility from order status and remaining quantities. View, search, track, and receipt actions are read-only. Confirmed pickup edits replace `orders[].pickupContact`, update `orders[].updatedAt`, and append `orderEvents[]` with previous and new contact. Confirmed cancellations update `orders[].items[].canceledQuantity`, `orders[].updatedAt`, and `orders[].status` when all lines are canceled. Append `orderEvents[]` with the affected line ids, quantities, reason, and refund estimate.
- [x] **Price match.** From a completed order line, offer **Request a price match**. From `/price-match`, show searchable request history with status and a **New request** action that starts from eligible purchase lines. Collect a Best Buy offer channel, current lower pre-tax unit price, quantity, and optional HTTP(S) offer URL. Keep the URL as evidence text. Validate remaining quantity, price, eligibility and duplicate requests, then review original unit price, offer unit price and requested difference. Confirmation appends one `priceMatchRequests[]` record with `submitted` status, purchase and line links, offer details, quantities, and requested amount. Display the request id and status in history immediately. The form models a post-purchase Best Buy price adjustment with a local review queue.
- [x] **Protection plans.** Show plans with product, plan number, coverage dates, and status. Filter by status and open plan details with the linked order, coverage terms, and claim history. Offer **Add protection** for a completed, eligible order line. Show the quoted fixed-term option, coverage dates, amount, and a confirmation. Append one `protectionPlans[]` record and a linked `protectionPlanEvents[]` `added` record. For active plans, **Request service** collects issue type and description, and appends a linked `protectionClaims[]` `submitted` record on confirmation. **Cancel plan** asks for a reason and confirmation, then updates the plan `status` and `canceledAt` and appends a `protectionPlanEvents[]` `canceled` record. Gate each action against current coverage and duplicate rules in the model. Surface every result through its plan detail and history.
- [x] **Returns.** Provide **Start a return** from an eligible order line and from `/returns`, with the order chosen first when opening from history. Show only available quantities for that order, a reason per selected line, and Store or Mail where eligible. Review line-level estimated refunds and the destination before confirmation. Append one `returns[]` `requested` record with order link, line ids, quantities, reasons, method, estimate, and store or local mail reference. The return detail shows its id, status, lines, estimate, and method instructions. While requested, **Cancel return** writes `returns[].status`, `canceledAt`, and `canceledBy`, and releases the reserved units. Derive return availability from records, including prior partial returns and cancellations.

## Scope

Begin with the synthetic account already signed in. The account area covers post-purchase support. Shopping, checkout, account creation, live carrier updates, payments, chat, real claim handling, and receipt delivery are outside this phase.
